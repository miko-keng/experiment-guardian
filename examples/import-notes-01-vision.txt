Experiment: Packaging Defect Vision Classifier

We evaluated whether a ResNet-50 model could detect dents and scratches on aluminum packaging more accurately than the previous classifier.

The training data was ImageNet-derived packaging imagery, dataset version 4.2, using an 80/20 data split. Images were resized to 224 by 224 pixels and normalized before training.

The model used PyTorch 2.4, batch size 32, learning rate 0.001, AdamW, and 20 epochs. The final model reached 92.1 percent accuracy on the held-out test partition.

The random seed was not recorded in the meeting notes. The team decided not to promote the model until that value is confirmed and the reproducibility audit is rerun.
